# Improvise recording tone and level — 2026-10-23

**PASS: implementation and local technical checks. PARTIAL: musical fit and speaker recognition; new comparisons await owner listening. NOT RUN: hosted worker publication and fresh song inference.**

The owner heard the earlier 30 blends and called them “okay but not perfect,” identifying different tone and volume between the recording and generated music. They requested processing that makes the recording belong in the song without making it sound like somebody else. [Exact feedback and reviewed-packet hash](evidence/improvise-tone/owner-feedback.json).

The shared local/Runpod arranger previously faded the raw recording and applied a global vocal-level target. It now applies a 60 Hz rumble filter, three broad tone adjustments bounded to ±3 dB against nearby estimated singing, gentle compression bounded to 6 dB with smoothed attack/release, and 6% short room reflections. The output level follows nearby active vocals and the backing at the insertion point, subject to existing headroom limits. Weak vocal references use neutral EQ and a backing-based level. Reflections end inside the existing phrase window; they cannot continue into the following singer.

The source remains the actual recording. There is no voice model, resynthesis, pitch shift, formant shift or time stretching. Word order, excerpt duration, placements and backing remain unchanged. This is a mix treatment, not a claim of perfect musical fit or proven speaker identity. Receipts now name the quantization check `processed_source_max_pcm_error`; its previous name could be mistaken for evidence about human identity. Old frozen receipts are retained.
