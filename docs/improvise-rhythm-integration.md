The shared local/Runpod arrangement path now includes the accepted approach. Source extraction records a final-word span only when it is complete inside the selected excerpt, 0.18–1.2 seconds long including margins, near the excerpt's end, and has ASR confidence at least 0.8. Uncertain timing omits the word repeat. The excerpt metadata must match its exact audio hash, sample rate and frame count before the arranger uses it.

When the estimated drum pulse has sufficient confidence, the arranger fits the complete excerpt to the nearest whole beat, within a duration ratio of 0.8–1.25. It uses the already-installed FFmpeg tempo filter without intentional pitch shifting or voice replacement. If a suitable word is available, another candidate adds that original word once, after a half-beat gap at 72% source gain. Transformed excerpts stay within five seconds. Every candidate still passes the existing singer-overlap, placement, source-use, six-second total-source budget and headroom checks.

Unaltered timing, beat fitting and a word reply are alternatives. Ranking balances candidate families and recent history; it does not force a repeated pattern or alternation. A weak pulse, out-of-range transform or unavailable tempo processor leaves ordinary source-inclusive arrangements available. If no safe arrangement exists, generation still fails rather than delivering a song without the recording. Original input hashes remain attached to the receipt. Selected transforms also record their ratio, word span, output hash and a local prepared-source WAV; history is saved only after a completed mix.
