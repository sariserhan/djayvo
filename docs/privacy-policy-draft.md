# Djayvo privacy policy — release draft

**PARTIAL. Not published.** This describes the local 0.3 prototype. Before public
release, the owner must supply the operating entity, support/privacy contact,
public policy URL and effective date, and review jurisdiction/age requirements.
These facts cannot be invented by implementation work.

Djayvo turns short sounds into music on your device. This version requires no
account and contains no advertising, analytics, cloud generation or automatic
upload feature. Djayvo does not send your recordings or generated music to its
developer. Apple and destinations you choose may process information under their
own policies; the app does not override device-level diagnostic settings.

Microphone access is requested only after you choose Record. A visible recording
state shows elapsed time. Recording stops after five seconds, when you stop it,
or when the app enters the background. Audio interruptions and removal of the
active audio route stop playback/capture; capture never restarts automatically.
You can import a recording instead of granting microphone permission.

Use recordings only with permission from the people who can be recognized in
them. Do not share private information or another person's voice without
appropriate permission. Take special care with children's recordings. The app
does not clone voices, identify speakers or imitate an artist.

Selected source audio, completed mixes, seeds, generation duration and creation
dates remain in the app's private library. This version excludes the library
from device backups. Completed sessions remain until you delete them or uninstall
the app. Temporary capture files are removed after import or at the next launch;
failed/cancelled generations are removed when cancellation is handled. An abrupt
termination can leave incomplete session files, which are withheld from playback
and removable with Delete all local data. The library allows up to 50 sessions.

Delete session removes that session's selected recording and generated files.
Delete all local data removes the app's entire private library and current clip.
External recordings you imported are unchanged. Unexported mixes will be lost if
the device is lost or the app is uninstalled.

Share opens the system share sheet with only the selected WAV. You choose the
destination. Djayvo does not attach the original file, transcript, source path,
location, score, seed or account identifier. Your voice may itself identify you.
Copies saved or shared elsewhere are outside Djayvo's deletion controls; manage
them through their destination. The app has no hosted feed or public library.

No training on recordings takes place. Any future cloud processing, training,
telemetry, account system or public community would require separate review,
updated disclosures and the appropriate opt-in controls before activation.
