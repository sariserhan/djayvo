Before you play

Tracks stay in this tab and are not uploaded or saved. Load local files up to 100 MB and 20 minutes. Pause a deck before replacing its track. Unchecking permission unloads both tracks.

For offline use, save the app in Voice studio → Privacy & credits, reopen this workspace, then load your local tracks. Reloading clears the decks. Keep the page open and your device awake; background playback and venue reliability have not been validated.

This preview provides manual playback and blending. Beat sync, headphone cueing, microphone capture and the AI performance channel are still to come. Opening the voice studio ends this session.
