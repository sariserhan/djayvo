    Local tracks stay in this tab; nothing is uploaded or saved. Files: up to 100 MB / 20 minutes. Pause a deck to replace it. Unchecking permission unloads your tracks; cleared instruments remain available.

    Enter each track’s BPM or tap it manually. Match tempo adjusts playback rate only; it does not align beats. Track loop repeats the complete file and may have a gap. Active pitch changes briefly pause and resume playback. The jog wheels seek through audio; they are not scratch emulation. Cue and hot cues are not quantized.

    Deck effects shape A and B independently, including prepared A. Tone softens the highs; Echo adds three fading repeats. Spacing is manual, not beat-synchronized; changing it bends the echoes’ pitch. Channel level, crossfader and Master also control echoes. Pausing one deck lets echoes finish. Reset effects, Unload and Pause all clear deck echoes.

    The piano covers A0–C8. Talk bass opens the computer-keyboard register at C2; octave buttons change it. Mouth shape changes its tone, not its pitch. Guitar keys use the cleared bank’s E2–G5 range. Sustain holds released notes; All notes off releases every voice. Leaving the page or keyboard focus stops held instrument notes. Tone softens the high frequencies; Echo adds fading repeats to keys and pads. Echo spacing is manual, not beat-synchronized. Changing spacing bends the echo pitch. Echo tails may continue after All notes off; Reset effects removes echoes, and Instrument level or Master at zero silences them.
