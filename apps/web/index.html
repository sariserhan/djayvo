Privacy & credits

Recording starts only when you press Record and allow microphone access. Use your own sound, or get permission from everyone whose voice is recognisable.

Your recording and music are processed on this device. This offline studio does not upload audio, train on it, or use analytics. The page downloads its engine and instruments when needed.

Finished mixes are saved in this browser, up to 10 sessions. Your original recording stays only in this tab’s memory. Delete removes a session from this browser; it does not remove files you downloaded. Browser cleanup, private browsing or storage eviction can remove saved mixes. Download anything you want to keep.

WAV downloads contain the mix only. Share only when you have permission. Musical quality is still being evaluated.

Offline studio

Save the app, instruments and CC0 practice tracks for offline use (up to 64 MiB). Your recordings, account pages and API responses are never added to this offline cache. Saved mixes stay in your separate browser library.
