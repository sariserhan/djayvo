Settings

Music

Requires internet. Music is loaded when you choose it. Your recordings and local tracks are never uploaded. Reload the studio after changing this setting.

Updates

Checks wait until your set is stopped. Available updates download after a check and install when you quit, or when you choose Install update. Save your work first.

Audio and controllers

Choose speakers and cue headphones in the studio’s Headphones section. Enable MIDI in Keys & pads. Microphone and controller permissions are requested when you use them.
