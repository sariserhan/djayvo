# Musical intelligence development

The shared C++ engine executes validated musical decisions. R0 reuses its rule
planner; R1 adds offline demonstration capture for future supervised decisions.

- [R0 foundation and evidence](../docs/r0-report.md)
- [R1 demonstration workflow, limitations and next gates](../docs/r1-demonstrations.md)
- [Musical architecture](../docs/improvisation_architecture.md)

R1 exports are evaluation-only. No reviewed expert corpus, trained planner or
new human quality result exists from this increment. Model inference/training
must remain outside the real-time audio callback and retain the data, quality
and spending gates in the master specification and learning addendum.
