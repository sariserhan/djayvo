Offline R1 dataset exports remain evaluation-only. Browser recordings are private,
unreviewed and not automatically admitted to that dataset. No reviewed expert corpus, trained planner or
new human quality result exists from this increment. Model inference/training
must remain outside the real-time audio callback and retain the data, quality
and spending gates in the master specification and learning addendum.
